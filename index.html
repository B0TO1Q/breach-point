Contract KR-0417 · Kidnap recovery

Client
Nordvale Timber Co.
Hostage
Daniel Okafor, survey engineer. Held four days. Last seen in a yellow hi-vis vest.
Target
Farmhouse on the valley road, one floor, about 18 by 13 m.
Pay
$48,000 on recovery. Ammo, medevac and incidents come out of it.
Authority
Local police signed off on a recovery. They will not assist.

Intel

  • An informant counts five or six armed men. One walks the yard.
  • A woman lives at the farm. Treat her as a civilian unless she's armed.
  • The front door has a deadbolt. The kitchen side door on the west wall is usually left unlocked, and it creaks.
  • One inside room has a reinforced door. Expect the hostage there.

Rules for the use of force

  • Armed kidnappers who are a threat: force authorized.
  • Anyone unarmed, surrendering or restrained: never.
  • Give commands first when you can V. Some people comply, some freeze, some pretend.
  • Keep your eyes on anyone with raised hands until they're cuffed F.
  • Every round you fire is reviewed after the mission.

Success means the hostage reaches the exfil flare on the south road, alive. Checkpoints save when you enter the house, find the hostage and free him, and every 45 seconds while nobody is shooting at you.
